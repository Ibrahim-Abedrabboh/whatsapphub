Complete Beginner's Guide

Welcome. This guide walks you through everything the Opal Hotel Social Media Growth Hub can do, one click at a time. If you can send a WhatsApp message on your phone, you can run this — no technical skills required. Take it section by section; nothing here can break anything.

1What This Is

The Opal Hotel Social Media Growth Hub is a website your team logs into to talk to guests and customers on WhatsApp — far more powerfully than from a phone. With it you can:

  • 📇 Keep an organized address book of your customers
  • 📣 Send one message to many people at once (a “broadcast”)
  • 💬 Chat back and forth with guests in a shared inbox
  • 🤖 Build a chatbot that answers automatically, 24/7
  • 🛍️ Take orders from a product catalog (optional)
NoteEverything happens in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge…). There's nothing to download or install.

2What You'll Need Before You Start

  1. The web address (link) for your Hub — bookmark it.
  2. Your login email and password — your administrator creates these.
  3. A connected WhatsApp Business number — set up once by an admin (Section 13). You don't need it to start learning.
  4. A modern browser on a computer (best) or phone.
TipUse a laptop or desktop for setup tasks like broadcasts and chatbots — there's more room to work. The inbox is comfortable on a phone too.
Warning — keep your password privateAnyone with your login can message customers as your business. Don't share it, and don't reuse a password from another site.

3Logging In

  1. Open your browser and go to your Hub link.
  2. You'll see a “Sign in” screen.
  3. Type your login Email, then your Password.
  4. Click the Sign in button.
Success looks likeThe page changes to a Dashboard with a menu across the top. You're in!
Common mistake — extra spacesIf sign-in fails, check for an accidental space before your email or password (common when copy-pasting).
Tip — light or darkUse the ☀ / ☾ toggle (top-right) to switch between the light cream theme and the dark black-and-gold theme. Your choice is remembered.

5Your Address Book — Adding Contacts

Add one person

  1. Click Contacts.
  2. In the “Add contact” form, enter a First name and a Phone with country code, e.g. +966 50 000 0000.
  3. Click Add.
Success looks likeThe new person appears in the contacts table below the form.
Common mistake — phone formatAlways include the country code. 501234567 won't work; +966501234567 will.

Add many at once (import a spreadsheet)

  1. On the Contacts page, find the Upload CSV option.
  2. Download the example file if offered — it shows the exact columns (a phone column and a name column).
  3. Put your customers into that format and Save as CSV (in Excel: File → Save As → CSV).
  4. Click Choose file, pick your CSV, and click Upload.
Warning — consent mattersOnly upload people who have agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp. Messaging people who didn't opt in can get your number blocked by Meta.
NoteCustomers who reply STOP are automatically removed from all future broadcasts.

6Grouping Customers — Lists

A List is a named group of contacts, like a folder (e.g. “VIP guests”, “Newsletter”).

  1. Click Lists.
  2. In the “New list” form, type a name and click Create.
  3. Add contacts to a list from the Contacts area.
TipLists are how you choose who receives a broadcast. Make a few sensible lists early — it pays off later.
Note — Lists vs SegmentsA List is a group you build by hand. A Segment fills itself automatically from rules. Beginners can happily stick to Lists.

7Message Templates (and why WhatsApp requires them)

Note — the “24-hour window”WhatsApp only lets you send a free-typed message within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Outside that (e.g. a first promotional blast), WhatsApp requires a pre-approved template. This protects people from spam.

A template is a message design that Meta reviews and approves before you can broadcast it. It can include blanks like {{1}} that fill with each person's name.

  1. Click Templates → New template.
  2. Give it a name, choose a language, and write the body. Use {{1}}, {{2}} as personalization blanks.
  3. Submit — it goes to Meta for review.
Success looks likeYour template appears as PENDING, then becomes APPROVED (minutes to a few hours).
Common mistakeYou can only broadcast with APPROVED templates. Be patient; don't re-submit repeatedly.

8Sending a Broadcast

A broadcast (or campaign) sends one template to a whole List at once.

  1. Click Campaigns → start a new broadcast.
  2. Choose a template (an APPROVED one).
  3. Choose your audience — a List or Segment.
  4. Fill the blanks — map {{1}} to each contact's name.
  5. Choose Send now or Schedule for later.
  6. Click Send.
Success looks likeA live progress bar showing Queued → Sending → Sent, with a count that climbs as it delivers.
Tip — start smallFor your first broadcast, make a tiny test List with just your own number and send to that first. Seeing it arrive builds confidence fast.
Common mistakeDouble-check the audience before you hit send — a broadcast can't be “unsent”.

9The Inbox — Chatting With Customers

  1. Click Inbox. Conversations are on the left; click one to open it.
  2. Type your reply in the box at the bottom and press Send.

Organizing (great for teams): mark a chat Open / Pending / Closed, assign it to a teammate, leave a private internal note the customer never sees, or drop in a saved canned reply.

AI helpers (if switched on): Suggest reply, Summarize, and Translate (Arabic ↔ English).

Note — you're always in controlAI suggests; nothing reaches the customer until you click Send.
Warning — the 24-hour window againIf a customer hasn't messaged in over 24 hours, WhatsApp blocks free-typed replies — reach them with an approved template instead.

10Building a Chatbot

A chatbot replies to customers automatically — perfect for FAQs, collecting info, or guiding a booking while your team sleeps.

  1. Click Chatbot → + New chatbot and give it a name.
  2. Add steps (building blocks). Hover any step button for a tooltip.
StepWhat it does
Send messageSends a line of text, then continues
Ask & waitAsks a question, saves the reply
Buttons / menuOffers up to 10 choices; branches on the pick
Branch on replyRoutes by keywords the customer types
DelayWaits a set time before the next step
AI answerAnswers from your uploaded knowledge documents
AI AgentHands off to a smart AI that chats until done
EndEnds the conversation

Drag steps to reorder. In Buttons/Branch steps, each choice's “→ go to…” dropdown picks the next step. Use {{name}} to insert the customer's name. Then set Status → Published and click Save.

Success looks likeYour bot appears in the list with a green PUBLISHED badge.
Warning — AI steps need setup“AI answer” and “AI Agent” only give real answers if your admin turned on AI and uploaded knowledge documents. The other 6 step types work regardless.
NoteA saved bot doesn't run by itself — connect it to a trigger next.

11Automation — Making the Bot Start

Automation rules decide when something automatic happens.

  1. Go to Settings → Automation → new rule; give it a name.
  2. Choose a Trigger: Keyword match (words in the message) or Outside business hours.
  3. Choose an Action: Send auto-reply text, or Start a chatbot (pick a Published bot).
  4. Save.
Common mistake — bot missing from the dropdownOnly Published chatbots appear under “Start a chatbot.” Publish it in the builder first.
TipA human can take over anytime — if you reply manually to a chat the bot is handling, the bot steps aside.

12Orders & Selling Products (optional)

If your business sells from a product catalog, the Hub can receive orders.

  • Customers send a cart from WhatsApp; it appears under Orders.
  • Each order moves NEW → CONFIRMED → SHIPPED → DELIVERED (or CANCELLED).
  • Moving an order forward messages the customer automatically (when the 24-hour window allows).
NoteAn admin must connect your catalog in Settings → Commerce first; until then the Orders screen stays empty. You can cancel an order any time before it's shipped.

14Troubleshooting

I can't sign in. Check for extra spaces; confirm the correct link; ask an admin to reset your password.

My broadcast won't send / the template isn't selectable. It must be APPROVED (not PENDING), and you must pick an audience.

I can't reply — the box is blocked. It's the 24-hour window; use an approved template to re-open the chat.

My chatbot doesn't start. Is it Published? Did you add an Automation rule with a “Start a chatbot” action pointing at it?

AI reply just says “a team member will follow up.” AI isn't switched on — the admin must add the AI key and upload knowledge documents.

A scheduled broadcast or a chatbot “Delay” never continued. These rely on the system's background worker; if it's down, timed actions stall. Ask your admin to check it.

Orders screen is empty. A product catalog must be connected in Settings → Commerce.
