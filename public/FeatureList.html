1

Effortless Onboarding & Setup

1.1One-Click WhatsApp Connection

Connect your official WhatsApp Business number through Meta's guided sign-up — no tokens, no developer dashboards. Approve access once and you're live.

1.2Guided Settings Hub

Every setup task lives under one Settings area with clear tabs. Your team gets fully configured without technical help.

1.3Multi-Brand Support

Run more than one business account, cleanly separated. Owner-level users switch brands in a click, and every setting stays isolated.

✅ Success: Most teams go from "just got access" to "sent our first message" in a single sitting.
📝 Note: Connecting your WhatsApp number is the one required step before sending — everything else can wait.
2

Contacts & Audiences

2.1Customer Address Book

Store every customer with name, phone, and custom attributes. Add people one at a time or import a whole spreadsheet.

2.2CSV Import

Upload a spreadsheet and the system cleans and validates every number, flagging problems before they cost you. A template shows the format.

2.3Lists

Group contacts by hand into named Lists — "VIP Guests," "Spa Members" — and target broadcasts precisely.

2.4Smart Segments

Build rule-based groups that fill themselves — "everyone tagged VIP." They update automatically, with a live preview of who matches.

2.5Automatic Opt-Out

When a customer replies STOP, they're instantly removed from all future broadcasts. You never police this manually.

⚠️ Warning: Only message people who've agreed to hear from you — non-consented sends can get your number restricted by Meta.
💡 Tip: Create a few sensible Lists early (by branch, by interest) — it makes every future broadcast faster to target.
3

Message Templates

3.1No-Code Template Builder

Design broadcast messages — headlines, body, buttons, media — in a simple form and submit them to Meta for approval from the app.

3.2Personalization Blanks

Drop in placeholders like a first name so every message feels one-to-one, even at scale.

3.3Rich Formats

Support image/document/video headers, buttons, coupon codes, and carousels — promotions that look polished.

3.4Live Approval Status

Each template shows Pending while Meta reviews, Approved when it's ready — you always know what you can send now.

📝 Note: WhatsApp requires templates outside the 24-hour reply window. This keeps you a trusted sender.
⚠️ Warning: You can only broadcast with Approved templates — approval takes minutes to hours; no need to resubmit.
4

Broadcasts & Campaigns

4.1One-to-Many Sending

Pick a template and audience and reach your whole list in one click.

4.2Send Now or Schedule

Fire immediately or schedule for the perfect date and time — a weekend offer, a booking reminder.

4.3Recurring Campaigns

Set a message to repeat on a schedule so routine touchpoints go out on their own.

4.4A/B Testing

Send two versions and let the numbers decide. The platform splits your audience fairly.

4.5Send-Time Optimization

Deliver at the ideal local time for each recipient — 9 AM in their own time zone.

4.6Live Delivery Tracking

Watch a real-time bar as messages move Queued → Sending → Sent, with per-message sent / delivered / read / failed.

4.7Trackable Links

Add a special link and see exactly who clicked — turning a broadcast into measurable interest.

✅ Success: You'll know within seconds whether a campaign is landing — and within minutes who's engaging.
💡 Tip: For your first broadcast, send to a test list of just your own number. Seeing it arrive builds instant confidence.
5

The Shared Team Inbox

5.1Two-Way Conversations

Every reply lands in a shared inbox your whole team works from — like team email, but for WhatsApp.

5.2Omnichannel Ready

Built to bring WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, and Messenger into one view, so you answer from a single place.

5.3Status & Assignment

Mark chats Open, Pending, or Closed and assign them to teammates so no two people reply at once.

5.4Internal Notes

Leave a private note the customer never sees but your team always does.

5.5Canned Replies

Save common answers as reusable snippets and drop them in instantly, personalized.

5.6Response-Time Awareness

Tracks first-response and resolution timing to keep service fast and consistent.

5.7Rich Media

Send and receive images, documents, audio, and video right in the thread.

📝 Note: WhatsApp allows free-typed replies only within 24 hours of a customer's last message — the inbox guides you to a template when that closes.
💡 Tip: Assign conversations as they arrive — the simplest way to stop double-replies and keep guests feeling cared for.
6

AI Assist

6.1Suggested Replies

Get a ready-to-review draft answer in your knowledge base's voice — you approve before anything sends.

6.2Conversation Summaries

Turn a long thread into a one-paragraph summary so anyone is instantly up to speed.

6.3Instant Translation

Translate messages between Arabic and English in a click — serve every guest in their language.

6.4Knowledge Base

Upload PDFs, Word, text, spreadsheets and the AI answers using your information, indexed automatically.

6.5Smart Message Tagging

AI labels an incoming message's intent and sentiment, helping you spot urgent or unhappy customers.

✅ Success: AI does the drafting and reading; your team keeps the judgment — nothing sends until a human clicks Send.
⚠️ Warning: AI features activate once your administrator adds an AI key on the server; until then they show a friendly fallback.
7

The No-Code Chatbot Builder

7.1Visual Drag-and-Drop

Build a chatbot by stacking simple steps and dragging them into order — no code, every step tooltipped.

7.2Eight Building Blocks

Send message, Ask & wait, Buttons/menu, Branch, Delay, AI answer, AI Agent, End — greet, qualify, answer, and route end-to-end.

7.3Tappable Menus

Offer up to ten quick-reply choices — "Book a room / Restaurant / Spa / Talk to a person" — and route on the pick.

7.4Human-Feel Delays

Insert a timed pause between messages so replies feel natural, not robotic.

7.5AI-Powered Answers

Add an AI step that answers from your knowledge base, or hand off to an autonomous AI agent until the goal is met.

7.6Publish & Reuse

Save bots as drafts, publish when ready, and edit anytime. Published bots are the ones automations launch.

💡 Tip: Start with a simple after-hours "Buttons" menu bot — it deflects common questions instantly and impresses guests with a fast reply.
📝 Note: A saved bot doesn't run on its own — you connect it to a trigger that decides when it starts.
8

Automation & Rules

8.1Trigger-Based Rules

Tell the system when to act — on certain keywords, or when someone writes outside business hours.

8.2Two Kinds of Action

A rule can send an instant auto-reply or launch a published chatbot — turning "book" into a full booking flow.

8.3Graceful Human Takeover

The moment a teammate replies, the bot steps aside so the human leads.

8.4Loop & Spam Protection

Built-in cooldowns prevent the system from ever replying in a rapid loop or over-messaging.

✅ Success: With a few rules, your Hub answers guests 24/7 — and hands off the moment a human touch is needed.
⚠️ Warning: Only published chatbots appear in the "Start a chatbot" action — publish yours first.
9

Commerce & Orders

9.1Product Catalog Connection

Link your Meta product catalog so customers browse and order right inside WhatsApp.

9.2Product Messages

Send single products or curated lists into a conversation to guide a sale.

9.3Inbound Order Capture

When a customer sends a cart, the order lands automatically with items, quantities, and totals.

9.4Order Pipeline

Move orders New → Confirmed → Shipped → Delivered, and the system messages the customer at each step.

📝 Note: Orders light up once an administrator connects a product catalog in Settings → Commerce.
10

Deliverability & Quality

10.1Rate-Limited Sending

Messages send at a controlled, safe pace that respects your WhatsApp tier and protects your reputation.

10.2Automatic Quality Guardrail

The platform watches your number's quality rating and pauses sending automatically if it's at risk — resume with a click.

10.3Reliable Background Engine

Broadcasts, scheduled sends, and timed bot steps run on a dedicated engine with automatic retries — nothing is silently dropped.

✅ Success: Your number stays healthy and trusted — the difference between messages that arrive and messages that get blocked.
11

Security & Privacy

11.1Secure Sign-In

Protected by short-lived, verified sessions with rotating, revocable refresh tokens — modern, bank-style auth.

11.2Role-Based Access

Team members get the right level of access for their role, keeping sensitive actions with the right people.

11.3Verified Webhooks

Every incoming Meta message is signature-verified, so only genuine WhatsApp traffic is processed.

11.4Consent Records

Opt-outs are stored and enforced structurally — a clean, auditable record of who agreed.

11.5Strict Data Separation

Every record is scoped to its owning brand, so one account can never see another's data.

⚠️ Warning: Keep login credentials private — anyone with access can message customers as your business.
12

Design, Language & Accessibility

12.1Bilingual Out of the Box

The whole interface works in English and Arabic, with full right-to-left layout.

12.2Light & Dark Themes

Switch between an elegant cream-and-gold light theme and a dramatic black-and-gold dark theme — your choice is remembered.

12.3Luxury Visual Identity

Serif headings, warm gold accents, and generous spacing give a refined, on-brand feel — it looks like Opal.

12.4Responsive Everywhere

Adapts cleanly to desktop, tablet, and phone, so your team can work the inbox from anywhere.

12.5Accessible by Design

Colors, focus outlines, and labels meet accessibility contrast standards — usable by everyone.

💡 Tip: Use the light theme for daytime desk work and dark in the evening — the toggle lives at the top-right of the app.
13

Platform & Administration

13.1Team Management

Add and manage teammates and permission levels from Settings, and reset passwords when needed.

13.2Self-Hosted & Private

Runs on your own infrastructure — your data stays yours, with no third-party middleman.

13.3Modern, Reliable Foundation

Built on proven technology, the Hub is stable, maintainable, and ready to grow with you.

13.4Built-In User Guide

A complete, plain-language user guide ships with the platform, so new members learn it independently.

✅ Success: Everything is in one system you control — from the first hello to a repeat booking, with no scattered tools to stitch together.

It lets a small team deliver big-hotel responsiveness — instant answers around the clock, personal service at scale, and campaigns that actually get read — while protecting your number's reputation and your customers' trust.

It's not just messaging software. It's how Opal delivers the true meaning of luxury — in every conversation.
