# Opal Hotel Social Media Growth Hub
## The Complete Feature List

### *"The True Meaning of Luxury"*

The Opal Hotel Social Media Growth Hub is a complete messaging and engagement platform built on WhatsApp's official Cloud API. It gives your team **one calm, elegant place** to reach guests, answer them personally, automate the repetitive parts, and turn conversations into bookings and orders — without a single line of code. This document walks through **every capability**, grouped by what it does for you.

---

## 1. Effortless Onboarding & Setup

**1.1 One-Click WhatsApp Connection.** Connect your official WhatsApp Business number through Meta's guided sign-up flow — **no copy-pasting tokens or hunting through developer dashboards**. You approve access once, and your number is live and ready to send.

**1.2 Guided Settings Hub.** Every setup task lives under one **Settings** area with clear tabs: connection, team, canned replies, AI knowledge, automation, flows, and commerce. **Your team can get fully configured** without technical help.

**1.3 Multi-Brand Support.** The platform is built to run **more than one business account** cleanly separated from one another. An owner-level user can switch between accounts in a click, and every contact, message, and setting stays isolated to its own brand.

> ✅ **Success:** Most teams go from "just got access" to "sent our first message" in a single sitting.

> 📝 **Note:** Connecting your WhatsApp number is the one required step before you can send anything — everything else can be set up at your own pace.

---

## 2. Contacts & Audiences

**2.1 Your Customer Address Book.** Store every customer with their **name, phone number, and custom attributes** (like city, loyalty tier, or language). Add people one at a time, or **import a whole spreadsheet** at once.

**2.2 CSV Import.** Upload a simple spreadsheet and the system **cleans and validates every phone number** for you, flagging anything that won't work before it causes a problem. A downloadable template shows the exact format.

**2.3 Lists.** Group contacts by hand into named **Lists** — "VIP Guests," "Newsletter," "Spa Members" — and use them to target broadcasts precisely.

**2.4 Smart Segments.** Build **rule-based groups that fill themselves automatically** — for example, "everyone tagged VIP" or "contacts in Amman." As contacts change, segments update on their own, so your targeting is always current. A live preview shows exactly who matches before you send.

**2.5 Automatic Opt-Out Handling.** When a customer replies **STOP**, they're instantly removed from all future broadcasts and recorded as opted-out. **You never have to police this manually**, and you stay compliant.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Only message people who've agreed to hear from you. Messaging non-consenting contacts can get your WhatsApp number restricted by Meta — the opt-out system protects you, but consent starts with you.

> 💡 **Tip:** Create a few sensible Lists early (by branch, by interest). It makes every future broadcast faster to target.

---

## 3. Message Templates

**3.1 No-Code Template Builder.** Design the messages you'll broadcast — **headlines, body text, buttons, and media** — in a simple form, then submit them to Meta for approval right from the app. No developer required.

**3.2 Personalization Blanks.** Drop in placeholders like a customer's first name so **every message feels one-to-one**, even when you're sending to thousands.

**3.3 Rich Formats.** Templates support **image, document, and video headers, tappable buttons, coupon codes, and multi-card carousels** — so your promotions look polished, not plain.

**3.4 Live Approval Status.** Each template shows its current status — **Pending** while Meta reviews it, **Approved** when it's ready to send — so you always know what you can use right now.

> 📝 **Note:** WhatsApp requires templates for any message sent outside a 24-hour reply window. This keeps inboxes spam-free — and keeps your business a trusted sender.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** You can only broadcast with **Approved** templates. Approval usually takes minutes to a few hours; there's no need to resubmit repeatedly.

---

## 4. Broadcasts & Campaigns

**4.1 One-to-Many Sending.** Choose an approved template, pick an audience, and **reach your whole list in one click**. The system handles the rest.

**4.2 Send Now or Schedule.** Fire a campaign immediately, or **schedule it for the perfect date and time** — a weekend offer, a booking reminder, a seasonal announcement.

**4.3 Recurring Campaigns.** Set a message to **repeat on a schedule** automatically, so routine touchpoints go out without anyone remembering to press send.

**4.4 A/B Testing.** Not sure which wording works better? **Send two versions and let the numbers decide.** The platform splits your audience fairly and shows you which performs.

**4.5 Send-Time Optimization.** Deliver each message at the **ideal local time for every recipient** — 9 AM in their own time zone — so your message lands when people are actually reading.

**4.6 Live Delivery Tracking.** Watch a **real-time progress bar** as messages move from Queued → Sending → Sent, with per-message status showing **sent, delivered, read, or failed**.

**4.7 Trackable Links.** Add a special link to any campaign and **see exactly who clicked** — turning a broadcast into measurable interest.

> ✅ **Success:** You'll know within seconds whether a campaign is landing — and within minutes who's engaging.

> 💡 **Tip:** For your very first broadcast, send to a tiny test list containing only your own number. Seeing it arrive builds instant confidence.

---

## 5. The Shared Team Inbox

**5.1 Two-Way Conversations.** Every customer reply lands in a **shared inbox your whole team works from** — like a team email inbox, but for WhatsApp. Read the full history and reply in a click.

**5.2 Omnichannel Ready.** The inbox is built to bring **WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, and Facebook Messenger into one view**, so your team answers from a single place regardless of where the customer wrote from.

**5.3 Status & Assignment.** Mark conversations **Open, Pending, or Closed**, and **assign them to specific teammates** so two people never reply to the same guest at once.

**5.4 Internal Notes.** Leave a **private note on any conversation** — "waiting on refund approval," "VIP, comp the late checkout" — that the customer never sees but your team always does.

**5.5 Canned Replies.** Save your most common answers as **reusable snippets** and drop them in instantly, personalized with the customer's name.

**5.6 Response-Time Awareness.** The inbox tracks **first-response and resolution timing**, helping your team keep service fast and consistent.

**5.7 Rich Media.** Send and receive **images, documents, audio, and video** right inside the thread.

> 📝 **Note:** WhatsApp only allows free-typed replies within 24 hours of a customer's last message. The inbox tells you when that window has closed and guides you to use a template instead.

> 💡 **Tip:** Assign conversations as soon as they come in — it's the simplest way to stop double-replies and keep guests feeling personally cared for.

---

## 6. AI Assist

**6.1 Suggested Replies.** With one click, get a **ready-to-review draft answer** to a customer's message — written in your knowledge base's voice. You always approve before anything sends.

**6.2 Conversation Summaries.** Turn a long back-and-forth into a **one-paragraph summary** so anyone picking up the thread is instantly up to speed.

**6.3 Instant Translation.** Translate messages between **Arabic and English** in a click — serve every guest in their own language without a translator.

**6.4 Knowledge Base.** Upload your real documents — **PDFs, Word files, text, spreadsheets** — and the AI answers customers using *your* information, not generic guesses. The system reads and indexes them automatically.

**6.5 Smart Message Tagging.** AI can read an incoming message and label its **intent and sentiment**, helping your team spot urgent or unhappy customers faster.

> ✅ **Success:** AI does the drafting and reading; your team keeps the judgment. Nothing reaches a customer until a human clicks Send.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** The AI features activate once your administrator adds an AI key on the server. Until then, they show a friendly fallback rather than a generated answer.

---

## 7. The No-Code Chatbot Builder

**7.1 Visual Drag-and-Drop Builder.** Build a chatbot by **stacking simple steps** and dragging them into order — no flowchart software, no code. Every step has a plain-English tooltip.

**7.2 Eight Building Blocks.** Compose any conversation from eight step types: **Send message, Ask & wait, Buttons/menu, Branch on reply, Delay, AI answer, AI Agent, and End** — enough to greet, qualify, answer, and route a guest end-to-end.

**7.3 Tappable Menus.** Offer customers **up to ten quick-reply choices**; they pick one and the bot routes them down the right path — perfect for "Book a room / Restaurant / Spa / Talk to a person."

**7.4 Human-Feel Delays.** Insert a **timed pause** between messages so replies feel natural and unrushed, not robotic.

**7.5 AI-Powered Answers.** Add an **AI step that answers from your knowledge base**, or hand off to an **autonomous AI agent** that converses back and forth until the customer's goal is met.

**7.6 Publish & Reuse.** Save bots as drafts, **publish them when ready**, and edit them anytime. Published bots are the ones your automations can launch.

> 💡 **Tip:** Start with a simple "Buttons" menu bot for after-hours — it deflects the most common questions instantly and impresses guests with a fast reply.

> 📝 **Note:** A saved bot doesn't run on its own — you connect it to a trigger (next section) that decides *when* it starts.

---

## 8. Automation & Rules

**8.1 Trigger-Based Rules.** Tell the system **when to act automatically** — when a message contains certain keywords, or when someone writes outside your business hours.

**8.2 Two Kinds of Action.** A matching rule can either **send an instant auto-reply** or **launch one of your published chatbots** — turning a keyword like "book" into a full guided booking flow.

**8.3 Graceful Human Takeover.** The moment a real teammate replies to a conversation a bot is handling, **the bot politely steps aside** so the human leads.

**8.4 Loop & Spam Protection.** Built-in cooldowns prevent the system from ever **replying in a rapid loop** or over-messaging a customer.

> ✅ **Success:** With a few rules, your Hub answers guests instantly, 24/7 — and hands off to your team the moment a human touch is needed.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Only **published** chatbots appear in the "Start a chatbot" action. If yours is missing, publish it in the builder first.

---

## 9. Commerce & Orders

**9.1 Product Catalog Connection.** Link your **Meta product catalog** so customers can browse and order right inside WhatsApp.

**9.2 Product Messages.** Send **single products or curated product lists** into a conversation to guide a sale.

**9.3 Inbound Order Capture.** When a customer sends a cart, the order **lands automatically** in your Orders screen with items, quantities, and totals.

**9.4 Order Pipeline.** Move each order through a clear status flow — **New → Confirmed → Shipped → Delivered** (or Cancelled) — and the system **messages the customer at every step**, when the timing window allows.

> 📝 **Note:** Orders and product messaging light up once an administrator connects a product catalog in Settings → Commerce. Until then, the Orders screen simply waits, empty.

---

## 10. Deliverability & Quality

**10.1 Rate-Limited Sending.** Messages are sent at a **controlled, safe pace** that respects your WhatsApp tier — protecting your number's reputation while still sending fast.

**10.2 Automatic Quality Guardrail.** The platform **watches your number's quality rating** and, if WhatsApp flags it as at-risk, **automatically pauses sending** to protect you from a block. You resume with a click when it recovers.

**10.3 Reliable Background Processing.** Broadcasts, scheduled sends, and timed chatbot steps run on a **dedicated background engine** with automatic retries, so nothing is silently dropped.

> ✅ **Success:** Your number stays healthy and trusted — the difference between messages that arrive and messages that get blocked.

---

## 11. Security & Privacy

**11.1 Secure Sign-In.** Access is protected by **short-lived, verified login sessions** with refresh tokens that rotate and can be revoked — modern, bank-style authentication.

**11.2 Role-Based Access.** Team members get **the right level of access** for their role, so sensitive administrative actions stay with the right people.

**11.3 Verified Webhooks.** Every incoming message from Meta is **signature-verified**, so only genuine WhatsApp traffic is ever processed.

**11.4 Consent Records.** Opt-outs are **stored and enforced structurally**, giving you a clean, auditable record of who agreed to hear from you.

**11.5 Strict Data Separation.** Every piece of data is scoped to its owning brand, so **one account can never see another's** contacts or conversations.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Keep login credentials private — anyone with access can message customers as your business. Never reuse a password from another site.

---

## 12. Design, Language & Accessibility

**12.1 Bilingual, Right Out of the Box.** The entire interface works in **English and Arabic**, including full right-to-left layout — so your whole team is comfortable, whatever they speak.

**12.2 Light & Dark Themes.** Switch between an elegant **cream-and-gold light theme** and a dramatic **black-and-gold dark theme** with a single toggle. Your choice is remembered.

**12.3 Luxury Visual Identity.** Serif headings, warm gold accents, and generous spacing give the platform a **refined, on-brand feel** — it looks like Opal, not a generic tool.

**12.4 Responsive on Every Device.** The Hub adapts cleanly to **desktop, tablet, and phone**, so your team can work the inbox from anywhere.

**12.5 Accessible by Design.** Colors, focus outlines, and labels meet **accessibility contrast standards**, so the platform is usable by everyone.

> 💡 **Tip:** Use the light theme for daytime desk work and the dark theme in the evening — the toggle lives in the top-right of the app.

## 13. Platform & Administration

**13.1 Team Management.** Add and manage **teammates and their permission levels** from Settings, and reset passwords when needed.

**13.2 Self-Hosted & Private.** The entire platform runs on **your own infrastructure** — your data stays yours, with no third-party middleman between you and WhatsApp.

**13.3 Reliable, Modern Foundation.** Built on proven technology (a robust database, a fast queue, and containerized deployment), the Hub is **stable, maintainable, and ready to grow** with you.

**13.4 Built-In Onboarding Guide.** A complete, plain-language **user guide ships with the platform**, so new team members can learn it independently.

> ✅ **Success:** Everything is in one system you control — from the first hello to a repeat booking, with no scattered tools to stitch together.
